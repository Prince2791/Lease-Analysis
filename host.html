Books and analyst: accuracy

Debrief

1. Same aircraft, different classification. Which leases are finance leases for the lessor, and which indicator tipped each one: term, PV test, or a bargain purchase option?
2. One contract, two numbers. For RVG deals the lessor counts the whole guarantee (para 70(c)); the airline counts only what it expects to pay (para 27(c)). For option deals each side judges "reasonably certain" on its own residual estimate. Find a deal where they disagreed.
3. Incentives. Did anyone take a cash incentive instead of a lower rent? It lowers the airline's ROU asset but not its liability, and the lessor spreads it over the term. Who prefers which, and why?
4. News. After the RBI hike, airlines' IBRs rose, so the same rent gave a smaller lease liability and more covenant headroom. Is a higher-rate world really "less leveraged"?
5. Structuring. Did lessors whose boards wanted finance leases use guarantees or bargain options to get there? Did capped airlines shorten terms or push rent into an option price? What should an analyst adjust?
6. Analyst desk. Ind AS 116 raised every airline's EBITDA and debt-to-equity on day 1 while year-1 profit fell. How did the 8× rent shortcut compare with the discounted liability for long versus short leases?
